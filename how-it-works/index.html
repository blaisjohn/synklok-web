Explainer · RME M-1620 Pro D

How Synklok works
in plain words.

Your M-1620 Pro D doesn't change its sample rate when the MADI signal coming into it does. Synklok watches the converter and changes it for you. Here's how that works, what you need, and where the limits are.

01 The problem 02 What it does 03 What you need 04 Settings 05 Good to know

01 · THE PROBLEM

The M-1620 doesn't follow on its own

Sample rate is how many times per second digital audio is measured — 44.1k, 48k, 96k and so on. Every device in a digital chain has to run at the same rate, ticking in step with one shared clock.

MADI is a single cable that carries up to 64 channels of audio between your MADI interface and the M-1620. When you change your session's rate, the interface switches, and so does the signal on that cable. The M-1620 Pro D doesn't. It stays at whatever rate it was set to — on its front panel or in its web remote — until someone changes it.

“The external clock must match the sample rate configured”

RME M-1620 Pro D User's Guide, section 11.2

When the two disagree you get clicks, dropouts, or audio at the wrong speed and pitch — until someone walks over to the rack and fixes it.

02 · WHAT SYNKLOK DOES

It asks, compares, and sets

The M-1620 has a remote-control interface built in, documented in RME's manual. Synklok uses it to talk to the converter directly — over a USB cable or your network. Nothing gets installed on the M-1620, and no audio ever passes through Synklok.

  1. 01
    ASK · TWICE A SECOND
    Synklok asks the M-1620 three things: what rate it's set to, what rate is arriving on its coaxial MADI input, and whether that input is locked — receiving a clean, steady signal.
  2. 02
    COMPARE
    If the MADI input is at a standard rate — 44.1, 48, 88.2, 96, 176.4 or 192 kHz — and the M-1620 is set to something else, they don't agree.
  3. 03
    SET
    Synklok tells the M-1620 to switch to the MADI rate, then gives it two seconds to settle before checking again. Most changes are done within a second or two.
ANY DAW · ANY MADI SOURCE

It watches the converter, not your software. Pro Tools, Logic, Cubase, Reaper, a live console or a MADI router all work the same way: if the rate arriving at the M-1620 changes, Synklok follows.

WARNS YOU

If the M-1620 and a locked MADI input still disagree after three seconds, the metronome in your menu bar turns into a warning triangle.

PAUSES WHEN ASKED

Need the converter to stay put? Switch off Auto-sync in the menu. Synklok keeps showing you the rates but stops changing anything.

RECONNECTS

If it can't reach the M-1620 — a cable pulled, the unit switched off — it tries again every three seconds, and can send you a notification when the connection drops.

03 · WHAT YOU NEED

Four things

  • ✓A Mac running macOS 14 Sonoma or newer — Apple silicon or Intel.
  • ✓An RME M-1620 Pro D your Mac can reach — by USB or over your network (see below).
  • ✓MADI arriving on the M-1620's coaxial input — the round BNC connector. Optical MADI and word clock are on the roadmap.
  • ✓Local network permission. macOS asks the first time you open Synklok. Even over USB the M-1620 shows up as a small network connection, so Synklok needs this either way. Missed it? System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.

Two ways to connect

USB REMOTE · DEFAULT

Plug a standard USB cable from your Mac into the USB port on the back of the M-1620. It appears at 172.20.0.1, which is where Synklok looks by default — nothing to set up. The port carries control only, not audio.

NETWORK · LAN

If the M-1620 is on your studio network, open Settings → Connection, turn on Use custom IP / hostname and enter its address (like 192.168.1.42 or m1620.local) — or pick it from the devices Synklok finds. Press Test to check.

04 · SETTINGS

Settings worth knowing

Click the metronome, then Settings… There are two tabs: Connection and General.

CONNECTION

Custom address

Point Synklok at a network address instead of USB. Test shows whether the M-1620 answers and what rate it's set to. RME devices found on your network are listed for one-click use.

NOTIFY

Notifications

A Mac notification when Synklok changes the rate, and another if the M-1620 disconnects. Both are on until you switch them off.

MENU BAR

Rate in the menu bar

Show the current rate — “96k” — right next to the metronome, so you can check it without clicking. Off by default.

SUPPORT

Copy diagnostics

Copies a plain-text report — versions, connection, rates, lock state and recent log lines — ready to paste into a message or a GitHub issue. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you paste it.

UPDATES

Automatic updates

Synklok checks synklok.app once a day and installs new versions in one click. Switch it off, or Check now, in the General tab.

STARTUP

Launch at login

Start Synklok with your Mac so it's always watching. You'll also find this switch in the menu.

05 · HONEST LIMITS

Good to know

  • Tested on the M-1620 Pro D only

    Other RME converters may have a similar remote-control interface, but Synklok hasn't been tried on them.

  • Coaxial MADI only, for now

    Synklok follows the coaxial MADI input. If your M-1620 takes its clock from optical MADI or word clock, it won't follow yet — both are on the roadmap.

  • High sample rates over MADI — still being confirmed

    MADI can carry 88.2 and 96 kHz in two ways: the “96k frame” or the older “S/MUX” method. RME's manual says the M-1620 can only detect the rate by itself with the 96k frame. We haven't confirmed yet how this affects Synklok. If it doesn't follow your session above 48 kHz, check your MADI interface's frame setting and let us know.

  • Coming from the old M-1620 Rate Sync script?

    If it's still installed, Synklok notices and shows you how to remove it, so the two don't fight over the rate.

  • A free fix until RME's arrives

    RME has said a future M-1620 firmware will follow its MADI input on its own. When that ships you may not need Synklok — until then, it does the job for free.

  • Private by design

    No accounts, no analytics. Besides your M-1620, Synklok only checks synklok.app once a day for updates.
