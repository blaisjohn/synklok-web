THE PROBLEM

Your session jumps from 48 to 96 kHz. Your MADI interface follows. Your M-1620 doesn't.

Now you've got dropouts, clicks, or audio at the wrong pitch — until you click through the web UI or the rack unit's front panel. Synklok closes the loop: when the MADI input rate changes, it tells the M-1620 to match. You don't have to think about it.

Built like studio gear.

Lives in the menu bar or system tray, launches at login, updates itself. You stop thinking about clocking.

STATUS

DIRECT

A native Mac and Windows app using the M-1620's own control API. No accounts, no analytics — besides your M-1620, it only checks synklok.app once a day for updates.

Install

Two minutes, once. After that Synklok checks for new versions daily and installs them in one click.

macOS 14 or newer
  1. 01
    Download Synklok
    Download the DMG. It's signed and notarized by Apple.
  2. 02
    Drag it to Applications
    Open the DMG and drag Synklok onto the Applications folder.
  3. 03
    Open it
    Launch Synklok from Applications and click Open when macOS asks about an app downloaded from the Internet.
  4. 04
    Allow local network access
    Click Allow when Synklok asks to find devices on your local network — it needs this to reach the M-1620. Missed it? System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.
  5. 05
    Find the metronome
    Synklok lives in the menu bar, not the Dock. Click the metronome for status, and turn on Launch at login.
Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  1. 01
    Download Synklok
    Download the installer for Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). This first Windows version is a test build and isn't code-signed yet; a signed version is on the way and test copies update to it on their own.
  2. 02
    Run the installer
    Open the downloaded Synklok-…-Setup.exe and click Install. It installs for your Windows account only, so there's no administrator password. Because it isn't signed yet, Windows will say “Windows protected your PC”: click More info → Run anyway.
  3. 03
    Find the metronome
    Synklok lives in the system tray by the clock. Windows 11 tucks new icons away: click the ^ arrow and drag the metronome onto the taskbar to keep it in view. Left-click it for status, right-click for the menu.
  4. 04
    Connect the M-1620
    Over USB REMOTE it connects on its own. On a network, right-click the metronome → Settings and enter the M-1620's address (shown on its STATE → network page).
  5. 05
    Turn on Launch at login
    Right-click the metronome and tick Launch at login so Synklok is always watching. To remove it later: Settings → Apps → Synklok → Uninstall.
